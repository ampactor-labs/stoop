THE PRESS

One source, imposed for paper. Change the format and the same pages re-flow onto different sheets.

The PDF is exact: right paper, no margins, nothing for a print dialog to argue with, and a file you can hand to a copy shop. Printing from the browser works too, at 100% scale, margins none, in the orientation the format names. Click any heading or body to edit it; edits save as you type. Fold a numbered test sheet before committing good paper — printers and folding hands vary, and if the numbers come out shuffled, hit SWAP FOLD and test again.

Everything fits on the paper.

HOW TO FOLD (one sheet, one cut)

  1. Hot dog fold. Fold in half the long way, crease hard, unfold.
  2. Eight panels. Fold in half the short way, then fold each outer edge back to the middle crease. Unfold everything.
  3. The cut. Fold in half the short way again and cut along the centre crease from the folded edge, across the middle two panels only. Stop at the quarter creases.
  4. The plus sign. Unfold, fold the long way with the slit along the top, then push both ends toward each other. The slit opens into a diamond, then a four-winged plus.
  5. Collapse. Wrap the wings around the front cover and crease. Eight pages, no staples. Read it once, then leave it somewhere.

Saddle-stitch formats print several sheets instead: print both sides, nest them in order, and staple twice through the spine.
