PAPER

Where the pages fold into a sheet.

PRINT IT

The PDF is the sheet at its exact size: print it, fold it, or hand it to a copy shop. A print shop that folds and trims its own wants the shop PDF, every page on its own with bleed and crop marks. Browser print needs 100% and no margins. The flyer has tear-off tabs. Riso plates are one greyscale PDF per ink, with a preview and a note on the stock, for a riso studio.

TEST THE FOLD

Print a numbered test sheet first and fold it. If the numbers come out shuffled, swap the fold and test again. A sixteen-page one-sheet fold is not offered yet: it has to be folded on paper before it ships.

How to fold one sheet into eight pages
  1. Hot dog fold. Fold in half the long way, crease hard, unfold.
  2. Eight panels. Fold in half the short way, then fold each outer edge back to the middle crease. Unfold everything.
  3. The cut. Fold in half the short way again and cut along the centre crease from the folded edge, across the middle two panels only. Stop at the quarter creases.
  4. The plus sign. Unfold, fold the long way with the slit along the top, then push both ends toward each other. The slit opens into a diamond, then a four-winged plus.
  5. Collapse. Wrap the wings around the front cover and crease. Eight pages, no staples. Read it once, then leave it somewhere.

A stapled booklet prints several sheets instead: print both sides, nest them in order, and staple twice through the spine.
